This example demonstrates the use and correctness of online momentum and kinetic energy budgets.
Turn on in diagnostics.opt and energy_budget.opt. This example calls the energy budget from
within random_output. In the future, a general output/diagnostics subroutine will be introduced
that will replace the role of random_output here.

Included are 2 matlab scripts; check_diags and check_ekb, which verify the momentum and energy budgets.
